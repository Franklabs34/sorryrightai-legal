Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

SorryRight AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

When you use SorryRight AI, we collect:

  • Full name and email address (when you create an account)
  • Situation descriptions you type to generate apologies
  • App usage data (number of apologies generated per day)
  • Subscription and payment status
  • Device type and operating system (for app compatibility)

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To generate AI-powered apology messages
  • To enforce daily usage limits based on your plan
  • To process your subscription payments
  • To improve the app experience

Artificial Intelligence (AI) Features

SorryRight AI uses artificial intelligence to generate personalized apology messages.

When you use our apology generation feature:

  • Your input text (situation, relationship type, tone) is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI for processing
  • This data is used solely to generate your apology message
  • We do not store your inputs on Anthropic's servers permanently
  • AI-generated content is suggestions only — review before sending
  • We are not responsible for how you use the generated messages

Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers in the United States
  • We use encryption to protect your data in transit and at rest
  • Only you can access your saved apologies
  • We never share your apology content with other users

Data Retention

  • Account data is stored until you delete your account
  • Saved apologies are kept until you delete them or your account
  • Daily usage logs are automatically deleted after 30 days
  • To delete your account and all data, email us at sorryrightai@gmail.com — we process within 7 days

Your Rights (USA Users)

Under applicable US privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Know what data we collect and how it is used

Children's Privacy

SorryRight AI is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
